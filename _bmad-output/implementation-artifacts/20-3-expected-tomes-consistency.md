# Story 20.3: Expected tomes stay consistent with the series total and Trash restores (closes #524)

Status: ready-for-dev

## Story

As a librarian,
I want the series edit and the Trash restore to stop leaving expected tomes in states their creation refuses,
so that the series page and the agent see a consistent series.

## Context

Found in the story 11-10 review (#524). `ExpectedTomeService` refuses to create a tome above a closed series' total or on a position a live title holds. Two paths still produce those states afterwards:

1. **Lowering the total by hand.** `SeriesService::update_series` (`src/services/series.rs:104`) compares the new total with the owned **count** only (`SeriesModel::active_count_titles`); a live expected tome above the new total stays. Auto-settle then reports `OutOfRange` for it, and MCP `get_series` lists it.
2. **Restoring a title from the Trash.** A title is soft-deleted (`TitleService::delete_without_volumes`: its `title_series` rows stay live), a tome is created at the now-free position (the holder check joins live titles only), then the title is restored: the position is held again by a title that is not the tome's. `TrashService::detect_restore_conflicts` (`src/services/trash.rs:126`) has no `titles` arm.

What exists already:
- **Story 11-11 shows both states**: `services/expected_tomes.rs::place_on_grid` returns them as `TomeToReview` (`ReviewReason::AboveTotal`, `ReviewReason::Held`), listed under "Expected tomes to review" on the series page (`templates/pages/series_detail.html`, `#expected-tomes-review`). That section **stays as it is**: it is where legacy rows and the restore case are shown.
- **Story 15-4 already applies the total rule to agent proposals** (`services/series_update_proposals.rs::check_total_rule` / `positions_in_the_way`): a `series_update` may not lower a closed total below an owned position nor a live expected tome. Only the hand edit lacks it. The hand edit keeps its own *owned* rule (count, not positions — 15-4 D5), unchanged here.
- **Lock order** of every expected-tome writer: the series row first (`ExpectedTomeModel::lock_series_in`, `SELECT … FROM series WHERE id = ? AND deleted_at IS NULL FOR UPDATE`, by primary key), then the tomes — creation (`create_in`), edit and delete (`lock_tome`), settle (`settle_matching` → `lock_series_ids_in`), proposal acceptance (`series_acceptance.rs`, `series_update_acceptance.rs`), the position edit (`services/tome.rs:660`).
- **The series form is a plain `method="POST"` page** (`templates/pages/series_form.html`, handlers `create_series` / `update_series` in `src/routes/series.rs`). Every refusal is an `AppError::BadRequest` worded with `t!()` **without locale** (instance language) and comes back as a bare 400 fragment with no page around it (epic 20, "What the issues did not say").

## Acceptance Criteria

1. **AC1 — the total rule (D7).** Saving the edit form of a series that ends **closed** with a total `T` while a live, **unsettled** expected tome of the series sits at a position above `T` is refused: nothing is written (row and `version` unchanged). The refusal names `M`, the highest such position (`series.total_below_expected`, `position = M`). This covers lowering the total and switching an open series to closed with such a total. A total equal to `M` is accepted. A **settled** tome (its settling title live and holding the position, the `settled_predicate!` of `models/expected_tome.rs`) never blocks. An open series is never checked. (Scope when neither type nor total changes: open question Q1.)
2. **AC2 — one transaction, series lock first (Rule #22).** `update_series` runs in one transaction whose **first statement** is `ExpectedTomeModel::lock_series_in` (primary key, the lock every tome writer takes first; no new `FOR UPDATE`). Inside it, after the lock: the owned count, the highest unsettled tome position (a plain read, no locking read), then the version-guarded `UPDATE`. A tome creation and a total edit of the same series therefore serialise: whichever commits second sees the other's committed row and is refused (`PositionOutOfRange` for the tome, `TotalBelowExpected` for the edit). A series soft-deleted meanwhile answers the not-found page.
3. **AC3 — refusals next to their field (Rule #3).** On any refusal of the series form — create and edit — the page is rendered again with status **422**, the values typed kept (name, description, type, total; the `version` the form carried), the message in the **request** locale:
   - `series.name_required`, `series.name_duplicate` under the name field: `<p id="series-name-error">`;
   - `series.total_required_for_closed`, `series.total_below_owned`, `series.total_below_expected` under the total field: `<p id="series-total-error">`;
   - the refused input gets `aria-invalid="true"` and `aria-describedby` naming its error id (only when the error is rendered), and `autofocus` (one field only: the full page is reloaded, as story 20-2 does);
   - the total group stays visible when the typed type is closed (`type_value` = the typed type).
   One refusal is shown at a time, in today's order (name required → duplicate → total required → below owned → below expected). A version conflict keeps today's answer (open question Q3).
4. **AC4 — restore with the same ISBN (D8, Rule #20).** After a title is restored from the Trash (`POST /admin/trash/titles/{id}/restore`, committed), each live, unsettled expected tome that carries the title's ISBN (ISBN-13 equality, the one-rule normalisation of `settle_matching`) at a position the title holds in a live series is settled with it (`mark_settled_in`), through the existing settle path with `attach = false` (series locks in id order, then tomes): nothing is attached anywhere. The restore answer adds one line per settled tome: `admin.trash.restore_tome_settled` (series name, position). Best effort: a failed settle is logged (`error!`) and never fails the restore; the tome then reads as in AC5.
5. **AC5 — restore onto a tome it does not match (D8).** For each live, unsettled tome still sitting, after AC4, at a position the restored title holds in a live series, the restore answer adds an information line `admin.trash.restore_tome_to_review` (restored title, position, series name) followed by a link "Open the series" (`proposals.open_series`) to `/series/{id}`, where the tome is listed under "Expected tomes to review" (`ReviewReason::Held`). The tome is not changed. One line per (series, position), ordered by series name then position (an omnibus title can hold several positions). Line style: open question Q2.
6. **AC6 — nothing else changes.** A restore of a title that touches no expected tome, and any restore of another table, answers exactly as today (the success line `admin.trash.restore_success` only, same panel re-render, same `HX-Trigger: modal-close`). A tome the title itself had settled before its deletion reads settled again by itself (the predicate follows the live title): no extra line.
7. **AC7 — tests.**
   - **Unit:** the pure total check (`total_below_expected(series_type, total, highest_unsettled) -> Option<i32>`: open → `None`; closed with `T ≥ M` → `None`; `T < M` → `Some(M)`; no tome → `None`); `SeriesFormError::field()` and `localized(loc)` for every variant in the four locales (no raw key, the position appears, twice for `total_below_expected`); the form template renders each error under its field with `aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby` and one `autofocus`, and none of them without an error; the restore lines (`settled` and `to_review`) escape the title and series names and carry `href="/series/{id}"`.
   - **DB integration** (`tests/series_total_tomes.rs`, new, declared in `tests/integration.rs`, using `crate::common`): lowering below an unsettled tome is refused through the web form (422, message under the total field, typed total kept) and the row and `version` are unchanged; lowering to exactly `M` passes; a settled tome does not block; open → closed with a total below a tome is refused; a tome on a held position (`Held`) above the total blocks too; each existing refusal answers 422 under its field (create and edit); the **race**: a second connection opens a transaction, `create_in` a tome at position 5 (series row locked, not committed); the edit lowering the total to 4 is started and waits; the second connection commits; the edit is refused with `position = 5`, the total is unchanged; then the reverse order (total 4 committed first, `create_in` at 5 → `PositionOutOfRange`); the **lock scope**: with a tome insert held open on series B (`lock_series_in` + `insert_in`), `SET SESSION innodb_lock_wait_timeout = 2` and an edit of series A succeeds (pattern: `tests/series_gap_wishlist.rs::a_send_on_one_series_does_not_wait_on_a_tome_write_of_another` on the 20-2 branch, `tests/proposal_series_update.rs`); the **restore**: equal ISBN → tome settled (`settled_title_id` = the title) and the settled line in the answer; different ISBN and no ISBN → tome unchanged, the to-review line with the series link; a title that holds no tome position → the answer is today's; a tome the title had settled before its deletion → no extra line.
   - **E2E** (`tests/e2e/specs/journeys/series-total-tomes.spec.ts`, new, `specIsbn("ST", …)`): the refusal case (a closed series the spec created, a tome named on a gap, the total lowered under it: the message is in `#series-total-error`, `#series-total` keeps the typed value and has the focus); the existing refusals next to their fields (whitespace-only name, duplicate name, closed with an empty total, total below the owned count); the restore case (as Admin: a title of the spec's series deleted from its title page, its position named as an expected tome, the title restored from the Trash, the information line read in `#feedback-list` and its link followed to `/series/{id}`, the tome listed under `#expected-tomes-review`); axe (`checkAccessibility`) on the refused edit page.
   - **Existing spec updated:** `tests/e2e/specs/journeys/series-expected-tomes.spec.ts` "lowering the total lists the tome under review" lowers a total under a tome through the form — the path this story closes. It becomes the refusal (or is removed in favour of the new spec's case); the review list itself stays covered by `tests/expected_tomes_web.rs::tomes_that_fit_no_gap_are_listed_for_review` (state seeded by SQL).

- **Sequencing (Rule #24).** 20-3 is developed **after 20-2 merges** and its branch is rebased on `main` with 20-2 in it. Shared file: `src/routes/series.rs` — 20-2 adds to `series_detail_page`, `SeriesDetailQuery`, `ExpectedTomeLabels` and the test module; 20-3 changes `create_series_form`, `create_series`, `edit_series_form`, `update_series`, `SeriesFormTemplate`, `form_template_labels`. Do **not** touch `src/routes/series_expected_tomes.rs`, `templates/pages/expected_tome_form.html`, `templates/components/series_gap_grid.html`, `templates/pages/series_detail.html` or `static/js/mybibli.js` (20-2's). `series-expected-tomes.spec.ts` is edited by both, in different tests.
- **Reuse 20-2's field-error pattern** (on its branch: `FormErrors::of` + `ExpectedTomeError::field()` in `routes/series_expected_tomes.rs` / `services/expected_tomes.rs`; the slots, `aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby` and `autofocus` in `expected_tome_form.html`; `rerender` at the GET address). `data-submit-once` is not needed here (a double submit of the series form is idempotent apart from the version, which refuses the second post).
- **Read view (critical for AC2).** The database runs `REPEATABLE READ`: a transaction's snapshot is taken at its **first non-locking read**. The series `FOR UPDATE` must therefore be the transaction's first statement, so the plain reads of the owned count and of the tomes, which follow it, see every tome committed before the lock was granted. Do not run `active_find_by_name` or any other read on the transaction before the lock (run the duplicate check on the pool, before `begin`). A plain read (not `LOCK IN SHARE MODE`) is used on purpose: a share-locked range read on the tome index next-key-locks the next series' first entry (#627, story 15-4).
- **No deadlock.** The edit takes one row lock (its series) and writes that row; every tome writer takes the series lock first. Nothing locks a title then a series here.
- **Settled = the predicate.** "Unsettled" is `NOT settled_predicate!()` (live settling title **and** a live `title_series` row at the tome's position), the same as `ExpectedTomeRow::settled_title_id` reads. A tome whose recorded settler was deleted reads unsettled and blocks; a tome on a position another title holds (`Held`) is unsettled and blocks.
- **Owned rule unchanged.** `total_below_owned` still compares with the **count** of live assignments (15-4 D5); a title above the total is not refused by the hand edit. Do not switch it to positions here.
- **Restore wiring.** `admin_trash_restore` (`src/routes/admin.rs:902`): `table` is validated against `ALLOWED_TABLES`; titles never take the conflict-modal pass (`detect_restore_conflicts` has no `titles` arm, kept so — D8 restores, never refuses). The success OOB is a `Replace` of `feedback-list` (`innerHTML`): concatenate the success entry and the tome lines in its `content`. `restored.item_name` is the title. Series name and title go through `html_escape` (the helpers escape `message` and `link_label`); `href` is built from the integer id.
- **E2E data.** `specIsbn("ST", seq)` with `seq` derived from `vcodeSlot()` (`helpers/vcode.ts`) so a CI retry on the same stack never scans its own earlier ISBN; series names run-stamped (`ST-…-${RUN}`, as `series-expected-tomes.spec.ts`); assert only on the spec's own rows (Rule #23). Title flow: scan the ISBN on `/catalog` (no V-code: a title without volume can be deleted), assign it on its title page (`#assign-series`, `#assign-position`, `#assign-series-submit`, see `series.spec.ts`), delete it from the title page modal, name its position on the series grid (`[data-name-gap="N"]`, then the expected-tome form), restore it from the Trash tab filtered to titles (`admin-trash-restore.spec.ts`: wait for the filtered panel and `expectHtmxProcessed` before clicking Restore). An `info` entry fades after 10 s and is removed after 20 s: read it and follow its link right away. `loginAs(page, "admin")` for the restore test (Admin only), `"librarian"` elsewhere; no `waitForTimeout`.
- **Native validation.** The name input is `required` (a whitespace-only name passes the browser and is refused by the server); the total is `min="1"` without `required` (an empty total with type closed reaches the server).
- `mem-guard` + the station lock for every heavy command; pass the rule to any sub-agent that compiles or tests. Never run `cargo fmt`.
