` is the navigation drawer (mock-up states "Mobile"): one DOM, one "Main navigation" landmark at every width, one language form, one theme toggle, no second copy of anything. Classes are mobile-first: the base utilities describe the drawer (`fixed`, from the left, `w-70` = 280 px, at most 85 vw, off-screen and `invisible` while closed — so neither focusable nor exposed), the `lg:` ones restore the sidebar (`lg:translate-none`: no transform at all from `lg`, which would otherwise trap the collapsed tooltips, `fixed` boxes, inside the nav). `nav-open:` (static/css/input.css) applies below `lg` while carries `nav-drawer-open`, set by `static/js/nav.js` when the menu button of `components/nav_bar.html` opens the drawer. Always expanded there (`sb-collapsed:` only matches from `lg`): no collapse toggle, no tooltips. The slide and the scrim fade are `motion-safe:` only (prefers-reduced-motion: no animation). `visibility` is transitioned on closing only (it stays visible while the drawer slides out); on opening the open state's transition list leaves it out, so the drawer is visible — and focusable — at once (a discrete `visibility` transition would keep it hidden at the start, and nav.js's focus() would miss). `#nav-drawer` wraps the scrim and the nav. While the drawer is open nav.js gives it `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"` and the accessible name `data-dialog-label` — so `static/js/scanner-guard.js` treats it as a modal surface (a scanner burst neither reaches `#scan-field` nor activates a drawer control) — and removes them on close (at ≥ `lg` it is never a dialog). Stacking: the scrim and the drawer are `fixed z-50` (templates_audit::fixed_surfaces_stack_above_sticky_nav): above the page and the `z-40` sticky bar; they come first in the document, so the later `z-50` surfaces (connection-lost overlay, JS toasts) paint above them, and modals reach the top layer. #} {%- import "components/nav_macros.html" as nav -%}
